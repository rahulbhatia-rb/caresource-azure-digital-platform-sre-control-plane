# Digital Platform SEV-1

1. Declare owner and customer impact.
2. Freeze unrelated changes.
3. Correlate latest deployment with Dynatrace/Splunk signals.
4. Mitigate using smallest safe action.
5. Roll back or fail over when health gates breach.
6. Restore service.
7. Capture timeline and postmortem.
8. Convert recurrence risks into owned corrective actions.
